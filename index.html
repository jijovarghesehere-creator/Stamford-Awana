Midweek Bible Curriculum

Where Kids Learn About Jesus, Build Friendships, and have Fun!! Because Kids Matter to God

Awana stands for Approved Workmen Are Not Ashamed, based on 2 Timothy 2:15. Through fun and engaging club nights, kids discover God's incredible love for them as they learn to know, love and follow Jesus.

AWANA was founded in 1950 in Chicago and is a nondenominational, Bible-based, Christ-centered club that welcomes children from all backgrounds, both churched and unchurched. Today, with an unwavering commitment to the Gospel, AWANA is being used to reach over 5 million kids weekly in 134 countries. The program gives children and youth from every background an opportunity to know, love, and serve Jesus for a lifetime. Click here to read AWANA's Our Beliefs

How Discipleship Works

Every Thursday evening is broken down into three dynamic segments designed to maximize engagement, build lasting mentorships, and deepen your child's understanding of Scripture.

1

Game Time

Every week, kids enjoy high-energy games that get them moving and laughing. As they play alongside friends and caring leaders, they develop teamwork, sportsmanship, confidence, and meaningful connections.

2

Handbook Time

Kids meet in small groups with dedicated mentors to recite memorized Scripture verses, discuss biblical truths, and track their progress through age-appropriate handbooks.

3

Large Group Time

Kids gather together for engaging worship, interactive Bible lessons, awards recognition, and practical practical life applications.

Clubs Offered

Preschoolers

Cubbies

Nurtures the spiritual potential of kids through highly interactive games, fun puppet modules, creative crafts, and critical foundational scriptural building blocks.

Ages: 3 - Pre K
Early Elementary

Sparks

Ignites curiosity about Jesus through chronological walk-throughs of the Bible from Genesis to Revelation, active memory testing, and high-energy square track games.

Grades: K - 2nd Grade
Upper Elementary

T&T

Truth & Training focuses on cementing relational truths, answering heavy faith questions, and building everyday practical disciplines of grace and personal prayer.

Grades: 3rd - 6th Grade
Middle & High School

Trek

Challenges students to consider their destiny in Christ and pursue a lifelong lifestyle of discipleship through deep biblical exploration and community.

Grades: 7th - 9th Grade
